8Beats is an internet radio station dedicated to video game music, featuring a rotating selection of soundtracks spanning retro classics, modern releases, indie titles, and fan favorites. The programming changes throughout the day, offering everything from energetic battle themes and upbeat overworld tracks to relaxing ambient music and nostalgic chiptunes. It's an excellent station for gaming, coding, studying, working, or simply enjoying the artistry of game composers.
