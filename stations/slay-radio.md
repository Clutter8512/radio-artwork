Description:
SLAY Radio is a dedicated 24/7 internet radio station specializing in music from and inspired by the legendary Commodore 64 computer, specifically utilizing the iconic MOS Technology 6581/8580 SID soundchip. The station broadcasts a continuous stream of classic SID tunes, modern studio remixes, community arrangements, and demoscene tracker tracks. Combining vintage 8-bit nostalgia with contemporary electronic production, SLAY Radio is the definitive soundtrack for chiptune enthusiasts, retro gaming fans, and demoscene purists alike.
