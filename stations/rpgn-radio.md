Description: RPGN Radio (RPGamers Network) is a 24/7 online video game music station dedicated entirely to role-playing games. The broadcast spans the golden eras of 16-bit and 32-bit classics up through modern cinematic masterpieces, spotlighting sweeping overworld themes, tranquil town melodies, tense dungeon crawls, and adrenaline-pumping battle tracks. Featuring full in-stream metadata support, it is the perfect soundtrack for deep focus, coding, studying, or reliving the epic storytelling of legendary RPG adventures.
